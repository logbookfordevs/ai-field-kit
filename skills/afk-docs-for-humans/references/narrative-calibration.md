# Narrative Calibration

Use this reference for tutorials, explainers, decision documents, product briefs, or articles whose pacing and narrative shape materially affect comprehension.

## Narrative Spine

Narrative is the path through the material, not a decorative voice. A useful technical spine is:

```text
context -> tension -> evidence -> decision -> consequence -> next action
```

The spine is an internal structure, not a required heading template:

- **Context:** what world is the reader entering?
- **Tension:** what changed, failed, became confusing, or is now worth deciding?
- **Evidence:** what do the code, product, data, or workflow establish?
- **Decision:** what was chosen, learned, or implemented?
- **Consequence:** what should the reader expect now?
- **Next action:** what should the reader do, review, test, or remember?

## Calibration

Keep narrative quieter in operational and review-oriented documents. Let it become more visible in tutorials, explainers, and articles where curiosity and pacing help the reader learn.

Useful narrative:

- orients before introducing detail
- keeps evidence close to the claim it supports
- varies emphasis so important facts stand out
- uses concrete examples and plain-English demystification
- ends with an action, consequence, or durable lesson

Overwritten narrative:

- delays technical facts to manufacture suspense
- turns ordinary work into a dramatic story
- makes personality compete with review or operational guidance
- adds sentiment without changing what the reader understands or does

The branch is complete when a busy reader can skim the headings, reconstruct the path, and find the evidence behind each consequential conclusion.
