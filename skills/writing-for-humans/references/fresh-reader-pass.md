# Fresh-Reader Pass

Use a fresh-context Cartographer as a lightweight reader proxy when the user explicitly requests a second reading. It can reveal friction the writer's accumulated context hides. This is not a writing collaborator, a rewrite pass, or human validation.

## Activation

Load and run this pass only when the user explicitly asks for one of these outcomes:

- a fresh-reader pass or second-reader validation;
- a Cartographer review of the writing;
- another agent's assessment of reader load, momentum, drag, ambiguity, gaps, compactness, or reader questions.

Ordinary requests to create, improve, revise, critique, review, proofread, or validate writing use the main skill's text inspection without delegation. When the user requests this pass but Cartographer or an equivalent fresh-context agent is unavailable, perform text inspection and state that the fresh-reader proxy was not run.

## Handoff

Give the Cartographer only:

- the finished draft;
- the primary reader;
- the intended reader exit;
- the genre and likely reading situation.

Do not provide source notes, research, passage maps, author rationale, or earlier discussion. Those materials contaminate the fresh read by answering questions the draft itself may leave open.

Ask for a read-only report under 200 words. Do not ask for web research, delegation, or a rewrite. Allow at most three findings, ordered by effect on the intended exit.

## Taxonomy

- **Reader load:** effort demanded by abstraction, technical density, jargon, missing inference, or working-memory burden.
- **Momentum:** how consistently that effort is rewarded with orientation, concrete understanding, resolution, or useful progress.
- **Drag:** effort that produces little or no reader progress.
- **Ambiguity:** two or more plausible meanings where the piece intends one.
- **Gap:** a missing prerequisite, inference, scope condition, connection, or answer needed to continue confidently.
- **Buried payoff:** value that arrives too late or is obscured by setup disproportionate to the reader's job.

Do not invent a numeric “energy score.” Judge whether the load is earned and identify the textual cause of friction.

## Output Contract

```text
Word count: N
Reader load: light | moderate | heavy
Load earned: yes | partly | no
Momentum: steady | uneven | stalled
Fresh-reader verdict: clear | revise

Findings (up to 3):
- [drag | density | ambiguity | gap | buried payoff] <location>: <reader effect>
  Smallest useful change: <cut | example | signpost | split | reorder>

Reader questions (up to 3):
- [blocking | non-blocking] <question the draft causes a fresh reader to ask>
```

A blocking question prevents the intended exit or makes a consequential interpretation unsafe. A non-blocking question creates hesitation or curiosity without defeating the piece. Questions may expose an ambiguous referent, a missing bridge or prerequisite, unclear scope, or an unanswered implication.

## Reconcile the Report

Treat findings as observations from one proxy reader, not commands. Check each against the author contract, evidence, genre, and intended exit. Preserve productive difficulty, deliberate ambiguity, voice, or suspense when it earns its cost. Make the smallest useful revision for material friction and do not claim human validation unless a human reader was actually observed.
